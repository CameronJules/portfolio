# Project Design System

## What is it for

Make building fun tools fast,
while keeping them good looking

## The core idea

I want my fun tools to have all my favourite parts of UI.
React first, Figma second and accessible by any AI agent.

## Why it matters

I want to focus on the output of the tool not the UI
This lets me move fast and not think about fixing UI later.


## What was the process

I still used figma. For collecting ideas and wireframing.
Claude and Codex for converting images and specs to components.

I write my specs in md files in Obsidian. 
(All these posts are also md files!)

I give my agents access to the folders or condense my
long specs into token optimsed prompts.
