# Lily Productivity

## What is Lily Productivity

A mobile app to make todo lists less boring.
Designed and created for my girlfriend.

## Why is this needed
Todo apps have no personality. Just words and tick boxes.
If you touch it daily it should be a good experience.

## Core Functionality
Frog buddies help group your tasks.
Earn coins by completing task and buy items for your pal from the store.

## Whats The Stack
IOS first, coded in swift.
Server for AI chats built with node.js
