# Acerola Ascii Art

## What is this

After seeing "I Tried Turning Games Into Text" by Acerola
I wanted to make the ascii shader myself but for web.

## Why do it
I wanted to use my personal design system.
This was a good single day project for that.

It also let me learn a bit about shaders and image processing

## Why I enjoyed it

it's always fun to do something new even if its been done before.
I also got to see my UI in action and how it keeps focus on the output not the UI.
